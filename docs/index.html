Local Democratic Information Infrastructure Stack

A data-driven ecosystem map showing the stack of projects and products that collect, process, and distribute civic information.

Scope & Inclusion Criteria

Purpose: This map visualizes the supply chain of democratic accountability, focusing strictly on the infrastructure needed to extract data from government halls (agendas, votes, debates) and process it into actionable power.

Rule 1 (Data): Must deal directly with public meetings, legislation, representative accountability, or the electoral process.

Rule 2 (Infrastructure): Must build tools, standardize data, or provide a scalable, tech-enabled process.

Rule 3 (Human APIs): General hyper-local activist groups are excluded, but major organizing bodies that provide scaled infrastructure for resident engagement (for example hosting debates or compiling voter guides) are included.
